(These four tickers and figures are made up, purely to illustrate the three status colors below.)

🎨 What the colors mean

The Momentum Bell Score color tells you how much attention a ticker is getting right now.

  • Green — above 100. High attention right now; this is the kind of move that tends to attract other traders and keep going.
  • Amber — 70 to 100. Building attention; worth watching, not yet as heated as green.
  • Gray/Neutral — below 70. Quiet for now; it qualified for the list but isn't drawing heavy attention yet.

Tap anywhere on a row to copy that ticker symbol, and the 🗞 icon for its latest headlines.
